# F-104 — /app/keys tells users free keys are "issued manually" while the backend self-serves them automatically

## F-104 | categoria: usabilidade | severidade: baixa | status: aberto
- Tela: `https://brazil.ambern.dev/app/keys` page text: "Don't have a key yet? Free keys are issued manually. Contact us to get yours." (button "Request API Key").
- Passos:
  1) Open `https://brazil.ambern.dev/app/keys`.
  2) Read the "Don't have a key yet?" section.
  3) Compare with backend `POST /v1/keys` ("Self-serve key generation for FREE plan") and CONTRACT.md §C ("FREE and STARTER activate automatically").
- Esperado: consistent story — either FREE is self-serve/automatic (contract §C) and the UI offers instant issuance, or it is manual; not both.
- Observado: the frontend says FREE keys are manual/contact-us, contradicting both the contract ("FREE activates automatically") and the live self-serve `POST /v1/keys` endpoint that already exists.
- Plain impact: A visitor who could get a free key instantly is told to "contact us" and wait — a needless drop-off / conversion loss and mixed messaging about how to sign up. Relatedly, F-101 means even a manually-issued key would not work in the browser today.
